# 关键摘录：NORIA-O × SID

> The `noria:Service` is equivalent to the `Service` object from the TMF 638 - Service Inventory API.

> Using the `noria:serviceType` relates to asserting the `CustomerFacingService` or `ResourceFacingService` Service subclasses notions of the TMF 638 - Service Inventory API.

> The `noria:applicationFunctionalDomain` is equivalent to the Domain concept of the ODA Functional Framework.
