# 摘录：TS 28.312 Annex C

> The TM Forum defines the structure of an intent as a list of expectations with each expectation containing the requirements, goals and constraints to be achieved. Expectations are defined in the Intent Common Model (ICM) Intent Expression TR290A [20] and the reporting is defined in the Intent Common Model Intent Reporting TR290B [18].
